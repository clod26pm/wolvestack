Disclaimer

Read this before reading anything else.

Compatibility is for entertainment and reflection. Astrology is not science. The compatibility scores, daily horoscopes, and AI conversations with Vela are not predictions, diagnoses, or professional advice. Don't make decisions about your relationships, your mental health, your finances, or your life based on what this app says.

1. Astrology is not science

The astronomical calculations behind your charts are mathematically accurate (we use the same NASA-derived ephemeris data professional astrologers use). What's interpretive — what those positions mean for your relationships — is a tradition, a framework, an art. Many people find astrology useful for self-reflection. None of it has been validated by peer-reviewed psychology or relationship science.

2. Vela is an AI

Vela is an AI assistant powered by Anthropic's Claude. She has been carefully prompted to read charts in a warm, useful way and to refuse predictions ("will he cheat?", "is she my soulmate?") and crisis advice. She can still be wrong. She can still misunderstand context. Don't treat anything Vela says as therapy, medical advice, legal advice, or relationship advice from a qualified professional.

3. We do not predict the future

Compatibility scores reflect chart patterns commonly associated with relationship dynamics. They are not predictions of whether a relationship will last, succeed, or fail. A 95/100 score does not guarantee compatibility. A 35/100 score does not mean a relationship is doomed. Plenty of low-score couples are happy. Plenty of high-score couples break up. The chart is a pattern; the relationship is the people.

4. We do not give relationship, medical, or psychological advice

If you're considering leaving a partner, getting back with an ex, navigating abuse, struggling with mental health, or making any decision of consequence — please talk to a real human qualified to help. A licensed therapist. A trusted friend. Your doctor. Crisis lines listed below if needed.

5. If you're in crisis

The 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (US) is free and confidential — call or text 988. Outside the US, findahelpline.com lists local lines. The US National Domestic Violence Hotline is 1-800-799-7233.

6. We don't show personal accusations

The app deliberately doesn't generate predictions about a third party's specific behavior — we won't tell you "your partner will cheat" or "this person is lying to you," because we can't know that and saying it would be irresponsible. We describe themes, dynamics, growth edges. Translation between "themes" and "what this person will actually do" is your job, not ours.

7. Personal responsibility

By using Compatibility you acknowledge that (a) you are 18+, (b) you understand astrology is interpretive, (c) you will not rely on Vela or compatibility scores for any decision of consequence, and (d) you accept full responsibility for your own decisions.

8. Reporting issues

If Vela said something concerning, hurtful, or wrong: please tell us at compatibility2@pm.me. We genuinely want to make her better.
