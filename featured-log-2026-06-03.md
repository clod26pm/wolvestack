## Quota mechanics — observation

The "Answers Remaining" counter is not strictly a calendar-day reset. It appears to be a **rolling cap of 3 outstanding pitches**: while a submission is still "In Review" (i.e., neither Selected, Published, Not Selected, nor Skipped by the journalist), it continues to count against the quota. That means quota only frees up when journalists actually act on a question — which on Connectively can take 3-7 days.

This contradicts the simpler "3/day refilling at midnight" model I was operating on. Worth A's attention: if pitches sit in review for a week, the effective ceiling is closer to **~3 pitches every 5-7 days**, not 21/week.

**Implication:** the scheduled daily task will frequently find 0 answers remaining if the prior batch hasn't been judged yet. Submitting daily is currently impossible on the free plan unless throughput at the editorial end accelerates.
