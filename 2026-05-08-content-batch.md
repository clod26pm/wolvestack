## 🔍 Trend Report

**Top 3 most timely findings:**

1. **Orforglipron (brand name *Foundayo*) is now five weeks into the market and the consumer narrative is settling into a sharper shape than the launch coverage suggested.** Lilly's small-molecule oral GLP-1 cleared the FDA on April 1, 2026 under the Commissioner's National Priority Voucher pilot — 50 days from filing to approval, the fastest NME approval since 2002 — with no food, water, or time-of-day restrictions and a label spanning obesity and overweight-with-comorbidities (ATTAIN-1: -7.5% to -11.2% at 72 weeks; ATTAIN-2 in T2D: -5.1% to -9.6%). Five weeks in, three things are now clear that weren't on launch day: (a) at $149–$299/month cash, Foundayo is materially undercutting injectable semaglutide and tirzepatide on price, (b) the actual real-world weight-loss number is converging around **~12%** versus ~15% for injectable semaglutide and ~20% for tirzepatide — meaningful but not best-in-class, and (c) the FDA has reportedly requested additional liver-safety data from Lilly post-approval. That last point matters: it's not a withdrawal, it's a signal that the hepatic-signal question detected during phase 3 wasn't fully closed at approval. Strong content angle for affiliate audiences pricing-shopping the GLP-1 category.

2. **Semaglutide failed the Alzheimer's trials it was running — and the result was published in *The Lancet* on April 24, 2026.** EVOKE and EVOKE+ together enrolled 3,808 patients aged 55–85 with mild cognitive impairment or mild AD dementia, randomized to oral semaglutide 14 mg flexible-dose vs placebo for 104 weeks. Primary endpoint was change in Clinical Dementia Rating – Sum of Boxes (CDR-SB). **Result: no difference between drug and placebo at week 104, on either the primary endpoint or the ADCS-ADL-MCI secondary.** Notably, the CSF biomarker substudy did show ~10% reductions in p-tau181, p-tau217, and neuroinflammation markers (YKL-40, neurogranin, total tau) — the molecular signal moved, the clinical signal didn't. Novo Nordisk discontinued the open-label extension and terminated the program. This is the most consequential negative GLP-1 result of 2026 so far, and it directly reframes the "GLP-1 helps everything" narrative that was dominant on biohacker TikTok in 2025. The honest framing: the molecule is real and useful, but extension to non-metabolic disease is not a free transfer.

3. **The first published human intravenous-infusion pilot study of BPC-157 is now in the literature — and it's been quietly underweighted on r/peptides.** Lee & Burgess, *Alternative Therapies in Health and Medicine*, 2025 (PubMed ID 40131143). It is genuinely small — 2 participants, a 58-year-old male and a 68-year-old female, both of whom had received prior IV BPC-157 — but the design is interesting: 10 mg IV in 250 cc saline over 1 hour on day 1, 20 mg IV in 250 cc saline over 1 hour on day 2, with a comprehensive comparative-bloodwork pull pre- and post-. Results: no measurable effects on cardiac, hepatic, renal, thyroid, or glucose biomarkers; well-tolerated; no adverse effects; half-life under 30 minutes. The "human IV safety data on BPC-157 doesn't exist" line has been the dominant framing on Reddit and on Eric Topol's *Ground Truths* — that line is now wrong by exactly two patients. It's not a phase 1, it's not powered for anything, and it doesn't establish efficacy. But it's real human IV pharmacokinetic and safety data that didn't exist eighteen months ago, and it deserves to be cited correctly when the "no human data" claim comes up.

**Other notable developments tracked but not headlined:**

- A 2026 series of bone-mineral-density papers (most recently the JCEM advance article *Skeletal effect of semaglutide and tirzepatide in patients with increased risk of fractures*, Cornell-Weill, March 2026; and PMC12544599) confirm BMD declines of -1.6% (lumbar spine), -1.8% (femoral neck), and -2.8% (total hip) over ~34 months on GLP-1/dual-agonist therapy with ~7% weight loss. Mechanistically these losses are largely proportional to weight loss, not direct skeletal toxicity — but they're meaningfully larger in non-diabetic users than in diabetic users, which matters for the cosmetic-weight-loss audience.
- TRIUMPH-4 readout (retatrutide, December 2025) remains the latest pivotal datapoint: 28.7% weight loss at 12 mg over 68 weeks plus 75.8% WOMAC knee-pain reduction in obesity-with-knee-OA patients. TRIUMPH-1 (general obesity) and TRIUMPH-2 (T2D) are still expected Q2–Q3 2026. NDA filing consensus is now Q4 2026.
- Mastercard's BRAM update GLB 11691.1 (effective 2025) is increasingly the practical reason peptide e-commerce sites are pushed onto crypto-on-ramp processors and high-risk underwriters. Affiliate-content audiences ordering from gray-market vendors are running into checkout-failure mode more than they were six months ago.
- A new narrative review (Vasireddi et al., *J Hosp Spec Surg*, June 2025; PMC12313605) of 36 BPC-157 studies 1993–2024 covering preclinical efficacy across muscle/tendon/ligament/bone models, plus only three pilot human studies, remains the cleanest bibliographic anchor for "the science is mostly preclinical" rebuttals.
- The *Pharmaceuticals* (MDPI) Sikiric et al. October 2025 reply to Józwiak et al. on BPC-157's angiogenesis and nitric-oxide mechanism is the most current mechanistic source for tendon/ligament-healing content. Useful primary citation when the audience is technical.
