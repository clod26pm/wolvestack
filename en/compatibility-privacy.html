Compatibility ✦

Privacy Policy

Effective May 9, 2026.

Plain-English summary

  • Your birth data and your saved matches' data stay on your phone. We never store them on our servers.
  • The only thing that hits our servers is the text of your conversations with Vela (the AI), processed in real time and not retained for training.
  • We don't sell your data. We don't track you across apps. We don't show ads.
  • You can delete everything by deleting the app. There's no separate account to wipe.

1. Who we are

Compatibility is an iOS app developed by Mintaka Media Group Inc. ("we," "us"), a company organized under the laws of the Commonwealth of The Bahamas. Contact: compatibility2@pm.me.

2. What we collect

On your device only (never sent to us):

  • Your birth date, birth time (if you provided it), and birth city.
  • The names, birthdays, and (if you provided them) birth times and cities of any matches you save.
  • Your conversation history with Vela.
  • App preferences (notification settings, etc.).

Sent to our servers (only when you actively use the AI feature):

  • Your typed message to Vela, plus a derived structured summary of the relevant chart data (planet positions, aspects). This goes to our Cloudflare Worker, which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. The response is returned to your phone.
  • A randomly-generated device identifier (an opaque UUID, generated once on first launch and stored locally on your device) is sent with each AI request and used only for per-device rate limiting (preventing abuse of the AI). It is not joined to your name, account, contacts, or any analytics; we do not use it to track you across other apps or websites.
  • This data is processed in real time. We do not log message content. Per Anthropic's commercial terms, content sent through their API is not used to train their models.

Sent to third parties for sub management:

  • Apple processes your subscription payment (we never see card data).
  • RevenueCat receives an anonymous user ID + your subscription receipt to manage entitlement state.

3. Geocoding (looking up birth city → coordinates)

When you enter a birth city, we look up its latitude/longitude and timezone. We try iOS's native MapKit first (no network call). If that fails, we send the city name to Open-Meteo's free geocoding API. We don't include any user identifier in this lookup; it's just the city name. Results are cached on your device.

4. Why we collect it

Legitimate interests: running the service, computing astrological charts, providing AI conversation.

Performance of contract: if you subscribe, we process the receipt to give you premium access.

5. Cookies and tracking

The Compatibility iOS app does not use cookies. It does not include any analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or cross-app tracking. There is no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, AppsFlyer, Adjust, or similar tooling in the app.

6. Your rights

Email compatibility2@pm.me with the subject "Data Subject Request" to exercise the rights below.

  • EU/UK (GDPR): access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. To erase data: simply delete the app — all on-device data is removed.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): right to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing (we don't sell or share for cross-context advertising), and non-discrimination.
  • Brazil (LGPD), Canada (PIPEDA), Australia (Privacy Act): equivalent rights honored on request.

7. International transfers

Our AI proxy is hosted on Cloudflare (global). The Anthropic API and RevenueCat are US-based. Transfers from the EU/UK are protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Anthropic) or Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Data retention

On-device data: until you delete the app or wipe it from Settings. Subscription receipt at RevenueCat: as long as your sub is active, plus their standard retention period. AI request payloads at our proxy and Anthropic: not retained beyond the request (no logs of message content).

9. Children

This app is rated 12+ on the App Store and is intended for users aged 12 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we will delete any related data.
